Look at modern software, and you will notice a depressing reality:

**Everything looks the same.**

Every B2B SaaS website features the same white-and-purple gradient, the same three-tier pricing cards with a highlighted middle card, the same floating avatars, and the exact same corporate copy generated by generic AI prompts: *"Supercharge your team's synergy with our unified platform."*

Underneath the hood, the situation is even worse. Websites that do nothing more than display text and four static images download 12 megabytes of JavaScript, lag on mobile devices, and set 40 third-party marketing cookies before you can read the first paragraph.

Software engineering has traded craftsmanship for an industrial assembly line of bloated component libraries, interchangeable templates, and bureaucratic agency layers.

We built **OTHR** because we believe digital software can be extraordinary.

---

## The Two Halves of OTHR

OTHR is not an advertising agency. We are not an offshore dev shop. We are an **independent creative engineering studio**.

We are defined by the collision of two distinct personalities:

### 1. The Rebel
We reject the sanitized aesthetic of corporate enterprise software. We believe typography should be bold, layout structures should be unconventional, copywriting should be confident, and visual identity should leave an indelible memory. If your digital presence looks like every competitor in your category, you are already invisible.

### 2. The Experimentalist
We don’t view software merely as business logic; we view it as interactive craft. We write custom WebGL shaders, compose algorithmic Web Audio synthesizers, build procedural 3D packaging visualizers, and explore the outer boundaries of browser technology. Play is not a distraction from engineering; play is how the next generation of breakthrough software is discovered.

---

## Our Operational Rule: Brutally Fast Underneath. Unreasonably Polished on Top.

High performance and radical visual originality are often treated as natural enemies:
- Designers complain that performance engineers kill their creative vision.
- Engineers complain that designers bloat the DOM with heavy animations.

At OTHR, we treat performance and aesthetics as **the exact same discipline**:

- If an interaction takes 400 milliseconds to respond, the design has failed.
- If a 3D centerpiece drops below 60 frames per second on mobile, it is rewritten or progressively enhanced.
- If an enterprise backend leaks database connections or drops batch jobs, visual polish is meaningless.

We write compiled languages (Go, Rust), leverage static edge networks (Cloudflare), use native web standards (Astro, TypeScript, standard Web APIs), and minimize third-party runtime bloat.

## Why We Build Our Own Products Too

Most agencies only build for clients. When the invoice is paid, they hand over the keys and move on. They never have to wake up at 2:00 AM when an edge certificate expires or deal with a customer struggling through an onboarding flow.

At OTHR, we believe true engineering credibility comes from having **skin in the game**.

That is why we conceived, built, and operate our own proprietary software products, beginning with **MockGen**, our browser-based 3D packaging visualization pipeline.

When we build for our clients, they don't get theoretical consulting from people who only make pitch decks. They get battle-tested software engineers who build, launch, and operate real production systems every single day.

We build things that work.
